01 — The problem

Broken tracking rarely announces itself. It shows up as numbers that don’t agree.

  1. 01

    GA4, ad platforms and CRM disagree

    Meta reports one number, Google Ads another, the CRM a third. Nobody knows which to trust, so budget decisions become guesswork.

  2. 02

    Signals disappear on iOS and behind consent

    Browser restrictions, ad blockers and declined consent remove conversions before they reach the platforms — unless tracking is built to recover what can legitimately be recovered.

  3. 03

    Bidding algorithms learn from the wrong events

    Duplicate purchases, missing values or misfired leads teach Smart Bidding and Advantage+ to find the wrong people. You pay for it on every auction.

  4. 04

    It breaks silently after launch

    A site release, a new form, a changed checkout. Tracking that worked last month stops — and it is noticed weeks later, in the spend report.
