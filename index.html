01 — The problem

Your ad platforms only see the form submit. Everything that matters happens after it.

  1. 01

    Ads optimise on form submits, not qualified leads

    Meta and Google count every submission as a win. The algorithm learns to find people who fill in forms — not people who get hired or sign.

  2. 02

    Junk leads get cheaper, good leads get rarer

    Spam, duplicates and unqualified applicants look identical to great ones in the ad account. Cost per lead drops while pipeline quality erodes.

  3. 03

    CRM and ATS outcomes never reach the ad platforms

    Qualified, interviewed, hired, won: these stages live in your CRM or ATS and never flow back. The ad platforms optimise blind.

  4. 04

    iOS and consent signal loss

    Browser restrictions, ad blockers and declined consent remove conversions before they are counted — unless tracking is built server-side to recover what can legitimately be recovered.

  5. 05

    Performance Max chases the cheapest conversion

    Given only form fills, PMax and Advantage+ find the cheapest inventory that produces them. Volume goes up, outcomes do not.
