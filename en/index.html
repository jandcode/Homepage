FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Isn’t AI-built work lower quality?

Not when it’s done properly. AI speeds up the build, but it doesn’t replace judgment: every change is reviewed by a senior developer, tested, and checked on a preview deployment before it goes live. Tracking is verified event by event. The goal is speed with the same rigor you’d expect from a senior team — not mass-produced output.

What does a project cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote after the free 30-minute call, so you know the price before work starts.

Do you work across time zones?

Yes. JDCODE is location-independent and has delivered projects for clients in Japan, Australia and Europe. We agree on overlap hours for calls; everything else runs asynchronously. Replies come in under 24 hours.

Who do I contract with?

You contract with JDCODE LLC, a US limited liability company founded by Jan Dorbath. Scope, price and payment terms are set out in a written proposal before work starts.

How do you handle data privacy and GDPR?

Tracking is set up consent-first (cookie banner and consent mode) and documented, so you can see exactly what is collected. Privacy requirements for your market are discussed in the intro call; I don’t provide legal advice.
